What this service does

The Company Website Research API accepts a single company website URL and retrieves publicly available pages from that website. It returns the text of those pages so that research and written summaries can be based on the company's own published information. It does not use search engines, directories, social networks or third-party data providers.

What data we receive

  • the company website URL submitted in the request
  • the API key identifying the calling application
  • standard request metadata (timestamp, network address of the calling service)

We do not request, require or intentionally collect personal data, and we do not receive the content of your conversation.

What data we store

  • Request logs — the domain requested, the outcome, timing and error codes. Retained for REPLACE_RETENTION_DAYS days. Logs do not contain retrieved page content.
  • Cached research results — cleaned text extracted from the public pages of the website that was researched. Retained for up to 14 days, then deleted automatically.

We do not store credentials, payment data, or special-category personal data.

Content retrieved from third-party websites

The service retrieves publicly accessible pages from the website whose URL is submitted. It respects robots.txt, identifies itself with a descriptive User-Agent, and does not attempt to access password-protected, private or paywalled areas. It never submits forms, logs in, or enters credentials.

If a page happens to contain personal data — for example a staff biography on a public "About" page — that data is processed only to return the page text to the requesting application, and is deleted when the cache expires.

Legal basis (where UK/EU GDPR applies)

Where any personal data is processed, we rely on legitimate interests: providing a research tool that summarises publicly published business information, balanced against the limited, public and business-context nature of the data.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties. Data is processed by our hosting provider solely in order to operate the service.

Your rights

Website owners

If you operate a website and do not want this service to retrieve your pages, add a Disallow rule for the user agent CompanyResearchBot in your robots.txt — the service honours it. You may also email REPLACE_EMAIL to request exclusion, and we will add your domain to a permanent blocklist.
