Privacy Policy

Company Website Research API · Last updated: 20 August 2026

Who we are

This service is operated by Ascendly. Contact: info@ascendly.one.

What this service does

The Company Website Research API accepts a single company website URL and retrieves publicly available pages from that website. It returns the text of those pages so that research and written summaries can be based on the company's own published information. It does not use search engines, directories, social networks or third-party data providers.

What data we receive

  • the company website URL submitted in the request
  • the API key identifying the calling application
  • the time of the request

We do not request, require or intentionally collect personal data. We do not receive the content of your conversation with any assistant that calls this service. Our application does not read or record the network address of the caller.

What data we store

  • Cached research results. The cleaned text extracted from the public pages of the website that was researched, held in a cache so that repeating the same request does not re-crawl the site. Successful results expire automatically after 14 days. Incomplete results expire after 3 days, and records of failed attempts after 1 to 7 days depending on the reason. Nothing is retained beyond those periods.
  • Rate-limiting counters. A per-minute request count held against a truncated one-way hash of the API key, never the key itself. These expire within two minutes.
  • Operational logs. For each request we record the domain that was researched, whether it succeeded, the number of pages read, timings, and a truncated one-way hash identifying which API key was used. Logs do not contain retrieved page content. Logs are written to our hosting provider, Railway, and are retained under that provider's standard log retention; we do not operate a separate log store.

We do not operate a database. We do not store credentials, payment data, or special-category personal data.

Content retrieved from third-party websites

The service retrieves publicly accessible pages from the website whose URL is submitted. It respects robots.txt, identifies itself with a descriptive User-Agent, and does not attempt to access password-protected, private or paywalled areas. It does not submit forms, log in, or enter credentials, and it does not follow links to other domains.

If a page happens to contain personal data — for example a staff biography on a public "About" page — that data is processed only to return the page text to the requesting application, and is deleted when the cached result expires.

Legal basis (where UK/EU GDPR applies)

Where any personal data is processed, we rely on legitimate interests: providing a research tool that summarises publicly published business information, balanced against the limited, public and business-context nature of the data.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties. Data is processed by our hosting provider, Railway, solely in order to operate the service.

Your rights

If you believe we hold personal data about you, contact info@ascendly.one to request access, correction or deletion. Because cached results expire automatically within 14 days, most requests are satisfied by that schedule.

Website owners

If you operate a website and do not want this service to retrieve your pages, add a Disallow rule for the user agent CompanyResearchBot in your robots.txt — the service honours it. You may also email info@ascendly.one to request exclusion, and we will add your domain to a permanent blocklist.

More about the crawler: https://company-research-api-production.up.railway.app/bot.

Changes

We will update this page and the "last updated" date if this policy changes.
