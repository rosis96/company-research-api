CompanyResearchBot

You are probably here because you saw CompanyResearchBot in your server logs. This page explains what it is and how to stop it.

What it does

CompanyResearchBot reads a small number of public pages from a company website — typically the homepage plus pages such as About, Services and Case Studies — and extracts the text so that it can be summarised accurately by the person researching that company. It is operated by Ascendly.

How it behaves

  • Requests /robots.txt first and honours it, including Crawl-delay.
  • Reads at most about 11 pages per visit, then stops. It does not crawl entire sites.
  • Uses at most 4 concurrent connections, with a delay between requests.
  • Never submits forms, logs in, enters credentials, adds to a cart, or attempts to reach private, paywalled or password-protected areas.
  • Never follows links to other domains — it stays on the site it was asked about.
  • Skips login, account, cart, checkout, privacy and terms pages entirely.
  • Identifies itself honestly. It does not impersonate a browser to evade blocking.

How to block it

Add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: CompanyResearchBot
Disallow: /

The crawler checks this before every visit and will stop requesting your site.
